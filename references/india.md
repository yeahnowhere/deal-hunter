## 4. Purchase Mechanics (inspect before you commit)

- **Open-box delivery (Flipkart, select pincodes)** — agent opens the package in front of you; inspect before accepting. **Never share the OTP/confirm before opening.** If missing/damaged/different, refuse on the spot (COD refund is immediate; prepaid refund follows seller policy). Card-on-delivery is not allowed with OBD — payment is made first. Consumer courts side with buyers on OBD violations, but evidence (unboxing photos/video, invoice, chats) is what wins.
- **GST invoice for warranty** — Indian brands void warranty without the bill/invoice. Always keep the tax invoice (Amazon/Flipkart issue one; input-tax-credit buyers need the GSTIN option). A "deal" without an invoice is not a deal.
- **Warranty registration** — some brands (phones, laptops, appliances) require online registration within a short window of purchase; check the deadline and register. Check domestic vs international warranty.
- **Trust Shield (Flipkart)** — free 30-day protection on large appliances (defects + accidental + liquid damage), 48h resolution; prefer listings that include it for high-value appliances.
- **Refurb vs new** — Amazon Renewed / Flipkart Refurbished / Cashify: check grading, refurb warranty term, battery health (phones). Refurb can be great value but is *not* new — score it as a separate tier.
- **Exchange / buyback** — Amazon/Flipkart exchange, Cashify, or OLX resale of the old device cut effective price further; festive sales often add an exchange floor-price bonus.
- **Return & restocking costs** — before choosing a platform, know the return reality: return window (Flipkart 7/10-day vs Amazon), restocking fee, who pays return shipping, and replacement-only (no refund) categories. A cheaper price on a platform with bad returns is a worse deal if it might need returning. On arrival, verify against the listing (model, variant, seals) within the window — do not let it pass unused.
- **Community verification cross-check** — before the verdict, confirm long-term ownership reality in communities (see `community.md`); the Indian market has strong brand-specific failure patterns that store reviews bury.

## 5. Payment Depth (the India levers that change the real price)

- **UPI cashback** — GPay/PhonePe/Paytm run frequent cashback offers and marketplaces run instant UPI discounts. Ask the user's UPI app and check current offers.
- **RuPay credit card on UPI** — the only cards that link to UPI (Visa/Mastercard cannot). Free for the cardholder (merchant pays MDR); P2M purchases only; some banks add 2FA (OTP) above ~₹5,000 (RBI 2026). Rewards vary — e.g. Tata Neu Infinity ~1.5%, BOBCARD Eterna ~3.75% on online+UPI.
- **Coins & reward points** — Amazon Pay coins, Flipkart SuperCoins, Tata Neu coins, Myntra Insider, credit-card rewards: all reduce effective price. Redeem strategically (coins often worth more on their home platform).
- **Cashback aggregators** — CashKaro, CouponDunia, GrabOn stack an extra % back. Verify the coupon is still live; cashback lands later and can expire.
- **Bank offers** — SBI (typically 10% on Flipkart/Amazon during sales), ICICI, BOB, Amazon Pay ICICI (5%). Exclusions matter: many exclude COD, RuPay-on-UPI, cashback cards, and some categories (e.g. grocery).
- Fold all of the above into the **effective-price** formula in `finance.md`.

## 6. Scam & Safety (India-specific)

- **Fake seller clones** — listings mimicking brand/authorized pages at lower prices; check seller name, age, rating, return rate.
- **Counterfeits** — rampant in headphones, chargers, cosmetics, fashion; "genuine product" badges aren't proof — check seller + invoice + seal + the **BIS/ISI certification mark** (mandatory for chargers, electronics, cosmetics; fakes often skip or forge it — verify the licence number on the BIS portal when in doubt).
- **Refurbished sold as new** — verify the listing state explicitly; a "new" price on a refurb unit is overpricing.
- **Prepaid vs COD** — unknown seller + prepaid = risk; COD with open-box inspection is safer for first buys. Balance against card offers that often exclude COD.
- **UPI/OTP scams** — never share OTPs, "payment failed — resend" links, or screen-share with "customer support". Real refunds never require the customer to "activate" anything.
- **Community scam checks** — r/IsThisAScamIndia documents Indian scam vectors, payment-gateway anomalies, and malicious storefronts; search the seller/platform/offer there before any prepaid order to an unknown seller.
- **Paid review farms** — bursts of 5-star reviews + generic text + recent accounts = manipulation flag; verified-purchase is weaker on some platforms, so check date spread and video/photo reviews.

## 7. Category Cheat-Sheets

- **Phones** — SoC + RAM/ROM, camera sensor (not megapixel count), software-update commitment (years), battery, 5G band support, build; for refurb check battery health + warranty.
- **Laptops** — CPU generation (not just model name), RAM soldered vs upgradeable, SSD type/expandability, display (panel, resolution, refresh), build/hinge, India service network.
- **Appliances** — **BEE star rating** (energy cost over life), capacity vs household, inverter compressor (AC/fridge), noise, Trust Shield (Flipkart), installation charges, warranty term, and **brand service/installation availability at the buyer's pincode** (service networks vary wildly by city — an online-only brand with no local ASC is a repair trap).
- **Groceries** — **Legal Metrology Act**: MRP printed on packaged goods is a legal cap — never pay more; compare per-unit (per kg/litre) across apps; check mfg/expiry dates; watch subscription/fulfilment fees.
- **Fashion** — fabric content, sizing fit (read reviews for size), return window, sale-season vs real price (Myntra/Ajio inflate MRP before clearances).

## 9. Dynamic & Surveillance Pricing (check before you trust a price)

Platforms can show **different prices to different people** — same product, same day. Before treating any single quote as "the price":

- **Logged-in vs incognito** — compare the logged-in price against an incognito/guest window.
- **App vs web** — marketplace apps and the website can quote different prices and coupons; check both.
- **Device/OS variance** — some platforms A/B price mobile vs desktop or new vs returning users.
- **Pincode variance** — fulfilment/warehouse availability changes effective price and delivery fees by pincode; check the buyer's real pincode.
- **Groceries specifically** — per-unit (per kg/litre) price is the only honest comparison; watch subscription/fulfilment fees and checkout-only price bumps.
- **Fake urgency** — "one-time offer" / countdown timers that reset are manipulation; ignore them.

If two checks disagree, log both and decide on the lower **verified** price, not the first one seen. (Note: this mirrors the 400-people grocery-pricing investigation — same product, materially different prices per shopper.)
